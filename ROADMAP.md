# Roadmap

## Done in this version: raw truth, the index, the terminal, the literature, external debt

Raw data with hash chain, curated layer, method-break and mechanism catalogues, the infographic check,
documentation in five languages (phase 1). Composite index of living conditions under a sealed
pre-registration with eleven dated amendments, a terminal of indicators with one homogeneous base per
cell and a reading by term in three descriptive layers, a literature layer found by a sealed search
protocol (four published rounds), and the external-debt and public-debt-cost series as the Central Bank
publishes them (phases 2 and 3). No estimates, no score of government merit.

## Next

* **Hosting**: the page (`output/raio-x.html`) is a static file; the domain and the host are the
  operator's decision. In-browser verification of the seal is a candidate once hosted.
* **Colab notebook** that verifies the hashes of the files it reads.
* **State-owned companies after 2009**: Petrobras, Banco do Brasil and Eletrobras from 2010 on, from the
  companies' own statements, one line per accounting basis.
* **Paid parliamentary amendments before 2014**: the Portal da Transparência base starts in 2014; the
  Treasury's yearly transfer files (28 to 38 MB each) would extend it, hash-only.
* **Literature round 11** when the OpenAlex coverage of Brazilian journals improves, or with a second
  index (Scopus or Web of Science) under the same sealed steps.
* **Index items that stop in 2015**: re-evaluated by amendment when the IBGE publishes an official
  retropolation of the PNAD Contínua or when it completes ten years inside a new base period
  (amendment 11).

## Not planned

Causal attribution of outcomes to governments; any score of merit; estimates, imputations or
extrapolations; ranking by intent.
