**Registered:** 2026-10-20T16:14:33Z (UTC), before any data of the blocks below was downloaded. **Amends:** `PREREGISTRATION.md`
(sections B and E) and amendments 01 and 02. Requested during review on 2026-10-20. Decisions already taken by
the requester and fixed here: candle colour = raw direction (green up, red down) with "lower is better" written in the
chart header; "discretionary spending" is the operational measure of loose spending; ministries are counted on
1 January; commissioned posts are counted; the term "orçamento secreto" is not used anywhere in the project
(parliamentary amendments are reported by legal type and by the rule of each year).

Everything below is **raw data as published**, year by year (monthly where the source is monthly), with vintage,
hash chain and the method-break catalogue. No score, no composite index, no attribution of cause: markers on the time
axis (laws, plans, crises, the pandemic, the Central Bank autonomy law) are shown on the same axis as the series and the
reader attributes.

## E. The piggy bank (block "porquinho")

R$ 100,000 held in cash from January 1995. Lines, all pre-declared:

1. purchasing power deflated by IPCA (primary), INPC, IGP-DI, IPC-Fipe, DIEESE cost-of-living index (1997-2019), Ipea low-income index (2007+);
2. the same amount in the savings account (poupança, BCB SGS 25) and at the Selic rate, deflated by IPCA: the opportunity cost of holding cash, and the regressive face of the inflation tax (cash minus savings);
3. relative measures: the amount in years of GDP per capita, in minimum wages, in DIEESE baskets;
4. four external references, each deflated by its own inflation: US dollar (BCB PTAX, US CPI), Swiss franc (BIS/FRED, Swiss CPI), IMF SDR, gold (LBMA, USD per ounce).

The inflation tax is never added to inflation in this block.

## F. Product prices (block "produtos")

Items bought by every income group with long official or research series: DIEESE São Paulo basket items (rice, beans, French bread, coffee, sugar, milk, oil, meat; monthly since 1959), 13 kg cooking-gas cylinder (ANP 2001+, regulated before), gasoline (ANP 2001+), residential electricity tariff, São Paulo bus fare. Measure: hours of work at the minimum wage and at the mean income of the top income decile (PNAD/PNADC, already collected) to buy one unit. Statement fixed now: no product has zero price elasticity; these are low-elasticity necessities and regulated prices, and the text says which is which.

## G. The terminal view

One indicator at a time (or two to four small multiples with the cursor synchronised by year), three panels: candles on the level (real candles only where the source is monthly or quarterly; annual-only series are drawn as change bars or lines), the old-method and new-method lines drawn together wherever they overlap, government bands and dated markers; below, green/red bars of the year's change; beside, a text that changes with the year under the cursor, taken from the catalogues (method breaks, norms, mechanisms, plans, laws). Library: a pinned UMD build of TradingView lightweight-charts.
